---
name: Customer billing rules
description: Product rules for Apna Dhandha customer accounts, discounts, shared payments, and credit limits.
---

- New team accounts and customer records require a mobile number.
- Quotation and invoice discounts may be percentage-based or fixed-rupee amounts; GST is calculated after the discount.
- Customer receipts support partial invoice payments and advances across invoices. Allocate receipts to the oldest due invoice first; keep any excess as an unapplied advance.
- Exceeding a customer's credit limit warns the user but does not block saving the invoice.

**Why:** These are user-approved product behaviors. The due-date allocation order keeps receipts predictable when a customer has multiple unpaid invoices.

**How to apply:** Preserve these rules in customer, quotation, invoice, and account-creation flows unless the user changes the product requirements.
